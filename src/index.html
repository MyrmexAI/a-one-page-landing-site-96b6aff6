About us

Hive Coffee started in a tiny Ljubljana kitchen with a second-hand drum roaster and a stubborn idea: good coffee should taste of its place and the people who grew it.

Today we are a small team that buys directly from farms and cooperatives we trust, roasts in small batches, and keeps everything simple. Like a hive, we work better together — with growers, with local cafés, and with you.
